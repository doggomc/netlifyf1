How FreeF1 live timing works

FreeF1 connects straight to the official Formula 1 timing feed while a session is running, so the tower updates within seconds of the cars crossing each timing line. You see the running order, gap to the leader, interval to the car ahead, last and best laps, all three sectors and the current tyre compound with its age in laps.

Colours follow the TV graphics: purple marks the fastest time of the session, green is a driver’s personal best. Race-control messages, track status such as yellow flags, the Safety Car or a red flag, and live track weather sit alongside the tower. Outside sessions, the page shows the final classification of the most recent session.

Tips

  • Keep this tab open next to the cockpit stream on desktop, or switch between completed weekend sessions above the tower.
  • Times, sectors, tyres and gaps are shown directly from the official timing data — nothing is simulated.

Frequently asked questions

Is F1 live timing free on FreeF1?
Yes. Live timing is completely free, ad-free, and needs no account on phones, tablets and desktops.
What shows when no session is on track?
Between sessions the tower displays the full final classification, best sector times, tyres, weather and FIA race-control log of the most recent session, and switches live automatically once the next session begins.
Is the timing ahead of the TV feed?
Timing data typically arrives within a couple of seconds of cars crossing each timing loop, which is often slightly ahead of an internet video broadcast.

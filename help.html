What device details are included?

If you leave the box checked, we attach your browser/OS identifier, screen and viewport size, language, time zone, online status, and this Help page’s path to the initial ticket only. We do not collect browsing history or use these details to identify you across visits.

Your name, category, messages, and any selected diagnostics go to a private Discord support channel. The browser keeps a ticket access key and a small local chat cache so you can return to this ticket.
